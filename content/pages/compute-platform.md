## Compute Platform

For a number of the lessons we will use hosted JupyterLab instances on
resources provided via the University of Chicago on clusters they 
manage or on the National Research Platform. This page describes how
you can sign up to use these resources and connect to them. You only
need to do this registration one-time.

### 1) Go to [https://connect.tempest.uchicago.edu/](https://connect.tempest.uchicago.edu/)

![CI Connect 01](/images/ci-connect-01.png)

### 2) Click "Sign in"

![CI Connect 01](/images/ci-connect-02.png)

### 3) Select "GitHub" and follow the authentication process (which may differ depending on how your GitHub account is set up). 

![CI Connect 01](/images/ci-connect-03.png)

### (You may be asked to authorize ManiacLab to access your github data for authentication. If it does, select "Authorize maniaclab".)

![CI Connect 01](/images/ci-connect-05b.png)

### 4) Select workshops and then “Join” codas-hep (and only codas-hep). Your request requires approval so will not immediately grant you access. The approval make take up to 24 hours. Meanwhile, do NOT click join for other groups, this may result in your request being lost. While your request is pending you will see a yellow clock like:

![CI Connect 01](/images/ci-connect-06b.png)

### Eventually when your request is approved you will see a green check mark when reloading this page:

![CI Connect 01](/images/ci-connect-07b.png)

### Once it is in that state, you are signed up, approved and ready to go. We will explain how to use this to access the actual compute platform at the school itself.  

### In case of problems - post a message to the #codas-hep-2026 channel in the IRIS-HEP Slack workspace
